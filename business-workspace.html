        This device

        Saved views

        Saved views live in this browser on this device. There is no verb that stores a partner preference yet, so a view you save here does not follow you to another phone or computer, and it changes none of your workspace preferences.

        Scope

        What this does not search

        This searches people, practices, buildings, deals, leads and vendors — the records the record layer can search by name. Tasks, documents and your Doc conversation history are not searched here, because no door exists that searches them yet. This page will not pretend an empty result for those means there is nothing to find.

        This search names real people, practices and deals, so on a shared or unlocked phone a passer-by reads those names at a glance — and what you typed stays in the address bar until you clear it. The words of a search are kept in this browser only when you save a view; nothing here is cached offline, and closing the page leaves nothing behind but the views you chose to save.
