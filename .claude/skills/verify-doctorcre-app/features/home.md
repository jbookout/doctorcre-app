# Home

Home shows current work and deal attention, with personal and team scopes and links to matching deal rooms.

## Sub-features

`home-scope`, `home-refresh`, `home-deal-open`, `brief-queues`, and the shared Morning brief dialog.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open `/` or choose Home in Primary navigation. Use Just me, Team view, Refresh Home, or a deal in the attention region.

## Gotchas

Home's Deals section shows pending assigned call tasks in `Call tasks to review`. Check the exact deal, assignee, text, date and expandable call evidence. Add Joe task or Add Dell task records that task; Skip dismisses only its proposal. Confirm the new action through the refreshed Calendar & tasks read. Team view also shows pending call email subjects, recipient references, deal links and evidence. Review draft opens that exact saved call in local Call Mode, even when the recorder points to a newer call. It creates no draft and resolves no proposal. The existing report requires approval before creating an Outlook draft, and Joe or Dell sends it. Just me omits email proposals because the producer supplies no assignee.
