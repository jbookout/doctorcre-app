What every surface holds

  • Five type sizes and two weights. Numbers use tabular figures.
  • The accent marks the primary action, the focus ring and one recurring marker. Nothing else.
  • A panel is one tone step off its ground with a hairline. No box sits inside a box.
  • Motion only on a state change, and only transform and opacity. Nothing loops.
  • No gradients, blur or glow. Sentence case; no tracked capitals.
  • Status is a word plus a shape or border, so it reads without colour.
  • Focus is visible and returns to the control that opened a popup, panel or dialog.
  • Saving is not confirmed. Refused keeps the input. Unknown is not failed.
  • Every clock time is AM/PM. Every due date is a calendar date with a picker.

References

  • Linear: calm neutral surfaces, one accent, quiet hairlines.
  • Apple HIG principles shape the state vocabulary; no native mandate.
  • Carbon palettes: categorical colour with a persistent legend, separate from status.
  • WCAG 2.2: contrast floor, the keyboard move path, the reduced-motion floor.
