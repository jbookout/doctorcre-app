Rules

What every surface must hold

  • Every action has a keyboard and click/tap path. A Move menu sits beside every draggable card.
  • Focus is visible and returns to the control that opened a panel or dialog.
  • A modal dialog and a pinnable nonmodal side panel are different components.
  • Saving is not confirmed. Refused keeps the input. Unknown is not failed.
  • Charts have a table or list equivalent with units, window and source.
  • Theme, density and motion are personal. Follow mode never changes them.
  • No partner ranking anywhere by default.

References

Seven references, seven dispositions

  • Apple HIG principles shape the state vocabulary; no native mandate.
  • HIG agent skills: platform distinctions kept; not installed.
  • shadcn, rareui, transitions.dev, beui: menu, chip and receipt interaction references; no framework rewrite.
  • lieflat-charts: one chart grammar with table equivalents; product charts bound to V5-R04.
  • 21st.dev, Magic UI: component references only.
  • Carbon palettes, C4 notation: categorical colour with a persistent legend, separate from status.
  • WCAG 2.2 dragging, pause-stop-hide and animation criteria: Move menu, motion pause, reduced-motion floor.
